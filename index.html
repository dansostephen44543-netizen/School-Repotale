How this portal works

A school-managed results portal inspired by the general result-checking workflow.

01

Teachers enter scores

The school administrator registers student IDs, names and photos. Teachers record subject scores and publication status.

02

Results are published

Only results marked as published can be viewed through the student checker.

03

Students check privately

Students enter their ID and result PIN to view the result available for that account.

Important: This is an independent demo, not an official WAEC website and not connected to WAEC systems. It cannot retrieve official WASSCE results.
